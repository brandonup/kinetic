Visual 3
Upload thinking, get a working agent
The pipeline that turns raw expertise (books, articles, transcripts) into a context-grounded AI persona — removing the technical setup barrier.
Step 1
📄
Upload Corpus
Drop in the expert's raw materials — books, blog posts, transcripts, frameworks, notes.
→ Raw documents indexed
▶
Step 2
✨
AI Extracts Identity
AI reads the corpus and generates the agent's name, voice, principles, and system prompt.
→ System prompt drafted
▶
Step 3
🧩
Frameworks Extracted
Named reasoning tools are pulled from the corpus — decision models, mental models, evaluation criteria.
→ Reasoning toolkit built
▶
Step 4
🤖
Agent Ready
A working AI persona that thinks like the expert — accessible in Kinetic or via MCP in any AI client.
→ Live agent, usable anywhere
💡

Tim's connection: This is exactly the "wrapper for open brains" — Kinetic removes the technical gap. No one configures vector databases or writes system prompts. They upload the thinking, review what the AI generates, and they have a working agent. The "consulting to make output useful" is the frameworks layer — named reasoning tools that fire automatically based on the question.
